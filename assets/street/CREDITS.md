# Road 2 Safety — street-art identity

Refreshed campaign identity and wide banner, 14 November 2026. The existing Road 2 Safety logo and supplied campaign poster informed the new artwork. Logo and banner were created with AI-assisted image editing; they are designed campaign artwork, not documentary evidence.

The actual Freetown photograph used by the website is **Freetown street**, photographed by **Erik Cleves Kristensen**, 13 May 2014. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Freetown_street_(13992704238).jpg . Licensed **CC BY 2.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/ . The served photo is resized and compressed; display uses cropping, grayscale and colour overlays. The banner is a designed collage referencing this same photograph. Nothing in this usage identifies anyone pictured as a person who uses drugs or as a Road 2 Safety participant, or implies photographer endorsement.

Logo master: `road-2-safety-logo.png` (transparent RGBA, 1254 × 1254). Web asset: `road-2-safety-logo.webp`. Banner master: `road-2-safety-banner.png` (2172 × 724); compressed web copy: `road-2-safety-banner.jpg`. The complete artwork is displayed without clipping embedded text. Header wordmarks and website/app copy remain accessible HTML.

Primary colours: ink #11130F, green #006D3E, red #DD2B1C, gold #FFC72D, paper #F2E8CF, Sierra Leone blue #168BD1.
